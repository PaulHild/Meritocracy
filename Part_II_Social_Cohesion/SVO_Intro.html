Task 5: Allocating Money

On the next page you will complete a short task about allocating money between yourself and another randomly selected person. The other person can be any other participant in this room. There will be 6 questions. In each question, your task is to choose one option out of a range of options which differ in how much money is allocated to you and how much money is allocated to the other person. There are no right or wrong answers, this is all about personal preferences.

  • If this task is selected for payment, one of your 6 questions will be chosen at random and your choice in it will be carried out: you receive the amount you allocated to yourself, and the amount you allocated to the other person goes to another randomly selected participant.
  • In the same way, one of the 6 questions of the participant who allocates to you will be chosen at random, and you receive the amount that they allocated to the other person.
  • Your earnings from this task are therefore the sum of the two: what you kept for yourself, plus what someone else allocated to you.
