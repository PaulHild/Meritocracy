Final Decision problem: Allocating Money

On the next page you will complete a short task about allocating money between yourself and another randomly selected person. The other person can be any other participant in this room. There will be 6 questions. In each question, your task is to choose one option out of a range of options which differ in how much money is allocated to you and how much money is allocated to the other person. There are no right or wrong answers, this is all about personal preferences.

  • If this task is selected for payment, one of the 6 items will be chosen at random to be implemented with a randomly paired participant.
  • If this task is selected for your partner, one of the 6 items from your partner's decision will be chosen at random to be implemented and you will receive the outcome of that decision.
