Payment Rule — Please read carefully

In this Part II, you will be asked to complete 6 tasks. At the end, one of your payment-relevant decisions will be randomly selected to determine your payment for this part. Each of them is equally likely to be chosen, so it is in your best interest to treat every task as if it counts.
