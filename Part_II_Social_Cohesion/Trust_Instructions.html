How It Works

Just like in the previous problem, you will interact with both partners — Partner A ({{ ingroup_tier_label }}) and Partner B ({{ outgroup_tier_label }}) — simultaneously but separately.

At the beginning, you receive {{ trust_ec }} ECs for each interaction.

Step 1 — Send
Choose how much to send to your partner (e.g., 0, 10, 30, etc.). The amount is tripled before it reaches them.
Step 2 — Receive and Return
For each amount your partner might send to you, decide how much of the tripled amount to return.
You will first make a decision as the Sender and then as the Receiver. Your partners will do the same. Your final earnings will depend on both your and your partners' decisions.

Your earnings as Sender: ({{ trust_ec }} ECs − amount you send) + amount returned to you
Your earnings as Receiver: (tripled received amount) − amount you return

💡 Total earnings per decision problem = Earnings as Sender + earnings as Receiver.

📝

Example with you as the sender

You send 30 ECs → Partner receives 90 ECs → Partner returns 40 ECs.

  • Your Sender earnings: (50 − 30) + 40 = 60 ECs
📝

Example with you as the Receiver

Partner sends 20 ECs → You receive 60 ECs → You return 20 ECs.

  • Your Receiver earnings: (60 − 20) = 40 ECs
