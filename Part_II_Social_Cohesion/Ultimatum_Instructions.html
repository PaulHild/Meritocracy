How It Works

You will interact with each of your partners separately — with your new Partner A ({{ ingroup_tier_label }}) and with your new Partner B ({{ outgroup_tier_label }}). The task is the same for both pairings.

At the beginning, you receive {{ dictator_ec }} ECs for each interaction. Then, there are two steps:

Step 1 — Propose
Choose how much to keep and how much to offer to your partner (multiples of 10).
Step 2 — Respond
For each possible offer from your partner (0–100 ECs), decide whether you would accept or reject that offer.
You will be paid for both steps and for both pairings.

That is, you will make one Propose decision (Step 1) for Partner A and another, separate, Propose decision for Partner B. Depending on what Partner A and Partner B have chosen in Step 2 (Respond), they will (individually) either accept or reject your proposal.

If your offer is accepted: The amount that you offered is transferred to the relevant partner. You keep what is left of your 100 ECs after your offer is subtracted.
If your offer is rejected: The 100 ECs are destroyed, and both of you earn 0 ECs.

The consequences of your own two Respond decisions (Step 2) for Partner A and Partner B are exactly the same. If you accept an offer, then you receive the relevant amount offered to you. If you reject an offer, then the 100 ECs of the partner who made that offer will be destroyed, and both of you earn 0 ECs.

Note that the timing for the Step-2 decision is perhaps not what you might expect. The computer will not show you the offer from either partner and then you decide whether to accept or reject. Instead, you make that decision in advance: In Step 2 you tell the computer for every possible amount offered whether you would accept or reject. Afterwards, the computer will check the actual amounts your partners offered and make the accept/reject decisions for you, as instructed by you in Step 2.

💡 Your total earnings = proposer earnings + responder earnings from both interactions.
