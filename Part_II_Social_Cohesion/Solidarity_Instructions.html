How It Works

In this problem, 2 out of the 3 group members are randomly selected to win {{ solidarity_ec }} ECs. The third group member receives nothing.

Before you know whether you win or not, you may pledge some of the {{ solidarity_ec }} ECs to the unlucky person. In other words, you may decide in advance that you will, in case that you do win, transfer a specific amount to the person who has not won.

🍀 If you are LUCKY
You receive: {{ solidarity_ec }} ECs − your pledge
😔 If you are UNLUCKY
You receive: pledge from member 2 + pledge from member 3

💡 The pledge is made before anyone knows who wins and who doesn't. Each person in the group faces the same situation.

📝

Example

Suppose you pledge 10 ECs and the other two members of your group each pledge 20 ECs.

  • If you win: You earn {{ solidarity_ec }} − 10 = 90 ECs. Your pledged 10 ECs are transferred to the person who hasn't won.
  • If you don't win: You receive the pledged 20 + 20 = 40 ECs from the other two group members. Since you didn't win, your pledged ECs are not taken from you.
