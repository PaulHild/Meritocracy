Three parts: what to expect

In this experiment, you will be performing at an intelligence test in various rounds. The experiment itself consists of 3 separate parts and a short questionnaire.
  1. First, you will familiarize yourself with the intelligence test in practice rounds. Then, you will play two rounds of the intelligence test.
  2. Second, you will participate in this intelligence test and compete against others with your performance across many rounds.
  3. Third, you will play various economic games with the others.
