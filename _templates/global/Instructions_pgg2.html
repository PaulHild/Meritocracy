This is the same “Transferring Tokens” game you played during the previous part.

You have 100 tokens in your Private Account and 300 tokens are in the Group Account.

  • Each token in your Private Account earns you 2 ECs.
  • Each token in the Group Account earns 1 EC for every group member.
  • You can transfer up to 100 tokens from the Group Account to your Private Account, or transfer up to 100 tokens from your Private Account into the Group Account.

Your earnings = (Private Account tokens × 2) + Group Account tokens

Examples:

Scenario Your earnings
Everyone adds 100 tokens to Group Account Private = 0, Group = 600 → 600 ECs
No one moves any tokens Private = 100, Group = 300 → 500 ECs
Everyone takes 100 tokens from Group Account Private = 200, Group = 0 → 400 ECs

💡 Keeping tokens in the Group Account benefits everyone equally. Moving tokens to your Private Account earns more for you personally but reduces the Group Account for all.
