Stage II: Transferring Tokens

Your task in this stage is to choose how to allocate "tokens" between your individual Private Account and a joint Group Account.

At the beginning of this stage, each group member has 100 tokens in her or his individual Private Account. In addition to that, there are 300 tokens in the joint Group Account.

  • You can either transfer the tokens from your Private Account to the Group Account (up to 100 tokens at most) or transfer tokens from the Group Account to your Private Account (also 100 tokens at most). Alternatively, you can also choose not to transfer any tokens.
  • All 3 group members make their transfer decision at the same time. As soon as everyone has done that, the computer will take stock of how many tokens there are in each Private Account and how many tokens there are in the Group Account.
  • For each token in your Private Account the computer will pay you 2 ECs. Likewise, the other group members will receive 2 ECs for each token in their Private Accounts.
  • For each token in the Group Account, the computer will pay 1 EC to EVERY member of your group.

Example 1: Suppose you and the other two members of your group do not transfer any tokens between the accounts. Then, you would receive 200 ECs from your initial 100 tokens in the Private Account (2 ECs per token). In addition, you would receive 300 ECs from the initial 300 tokens in the joint Group Account (1 EC per token). In total, you would receive 500 ECs.

Example 2: Suppose you and the other two members of your group all transfer their initial 100 Private Account tokens to the Group Account. Then, the Group Account balance would be 300 + 3x100 = 600 tokens. Then, you would receive 0 ECs from your Private Account and, 600 600 ECs from the group Group Account.

Example 3: Suppose that you transfer your initial 100 Private Account tokens to the Group Account, whereas the other two group members each transfer 100 tokens from the Group Account to their Private Accounts. Then, the Group Account balance would be 300 + 100 – 2x100 = 200 tokens. You would therefore receive 0 ECs from your Private Account and 200 ECs from the Group Account.

Example 4: Suppose that the other two group members transfer their initial 100 Private Account tokens to the Group Account, whereas you transfer 100 tokens from the Group Account to your Private Account. Then, your Private Account balance would be 100 + 100 = 200, and the Group Account balance would be 300 + 2x100 – 100 = 400 tokens. You would therefore receive 400 ECs from your Private Account and 400 ECs from the Group Account (800 ECs in total).

Earnings

  • Your earnings from the Production Stage accumulate across all {{ C.Economy_num_rounds }} rounds.
  • For the Transferring Tokens Stage, only one of the {{ C.Economy_num_rounds }} rounds will be randomly chosen at the end to determine your actual payoff from this stage — the other rounds' decisions will not affect your earnings.

At the end of the experiment, all ECs are converted to EUR at the rate conversion rate of {{ C.EC_exchange_rate }} EC = 1 EUR.

For example, if you earn 1500 ECs you will be paid 15 EUR.
