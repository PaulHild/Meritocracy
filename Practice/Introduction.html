First rounds and grouping

🧪

First two rounds

  • On the next few pages, you will play two rounds of the Intelligence Test.
    • In each question, for the first 3 seconds, you are not able to submit an answer.
  • In both of these rounds, each correct answer will earn you {{C.Practice_ECs}} ECs.
    • Remember that accumulated ECs will be converted to Euros at the end.
👥

Grouping

  • After the first two rounds, you will be grouped with 2 other participants.
  • Grouping is based on how you perform in these two rounds.
  • At this point, we will not explain how the grouping works, but you should know that it is in your interest to perform at your best.
  • In other words, in addition to earning you ECs right now, your performance in these rounds affect your earnings later in the experiment.
  • It is in your best interest to perform as well as you can.
