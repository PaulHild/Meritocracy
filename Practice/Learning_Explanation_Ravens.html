Matrix Reasoning

You will see a pattern of shapes arranged in a grid, with one piece missing in the bottom-right corner.

Your task is to look at how the shapes change across rows and columns, then choose which of the 4 options (A–D) correctly completes the pattern.

In this learning trial you will answer 3 questions to get a feel for the task.

Example Question
[Explanation] In the first two rows, the same shape and pattern repeats itself. In the bottom row, there are two pentagrams arranged in a particular way. Which of the 4 options (A–D) completes the bottom row? D cannot be correct because it arranges the pentagrams differently. C also breaks the pattern because it varies the pentagrams’ sizes. B uses different colors. Therefore, the correct answer is A.
